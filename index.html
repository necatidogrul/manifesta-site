Questions

What is Manifesta?

An iOS manifestation app built around a closing ritual: release a wish cinematically, see it daily, plan for obstacles, and celebrate when it comes true.

Is Manifesta free?

Yes — free to download and use. Premium unlocks unlimited wishes, Universe Sessions, unlimited voice affirmations, all themes and the lock-screen candle.

How is it different from other manifestation apps?

Most apps stop at "write it down." Manifesta closes the loop: cinematic rituals, ambient universes, your own voice, and a galaxy that celebrates fulfilled wishes with you.

Does manifestation work?

Manifesta is a tool for reflection and inspiration — not a guarantee. Its backbone is behavioral science: name the obstacle, make a tiny plan.
