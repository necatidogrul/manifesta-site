# Manifesta — Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

> Last updated / Son güncelleme: 24 September 2026
> https://necatidogrul.github.io/manifesta-site/privacy.html

## In short (EN)

- There is no account. Name, email and phone number are never asked for and are not known.
- Wishes, obstacle-and-plan notes, check-ins and voice recordings stay **on the device**. There
  is no iCloud sync and no server of ours holding them.
- The only third-party service the app talks to is **RevenueCat**, and only about subscriptions.
- **No tracking.** No ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no IDFA. The App
  Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown because there is nothing to ask for.
- Data is not sold and not passed to data brokers.

## 2. What stays on the device
Stored in a local database inside the app's own App Group container, never mirrored to iCloud
or to any server:

- **Wishes and intentions** — wish text and the WOOP fields (desired outcome, obstacle, small
  plan), category, chosen ritual, state, creation date, any Time Capsule opening date and the
  gratitude note added when a wish is realised.
- **Voice affirmations** — the title typed and the audio file itself, written as an ordinary
  audio file inside the app's container.
- **Check-ins** — the daily closeness rating (1–5) and any note written with it.
- **369 method sessions** — the sessions and the lines written in them.
- **Settings and counters** — reminder time, onboarding category, streak counters, and the
  bookkeeping that decides when not to show the paywall or the rating prompt again.

There is no account and no profile on any server. Anyone holding the unlocked phone can read
the wishes; that is the trade-off of keeping everything local.

## 3. What leaves the device

### 3.1 Subscriptions
Manifesta uses **RevenueCat** to manage the Premium subscription. Sent to
`api.revenuecat.com` on launch and on purchase, restore or renewal:

- the **identifierForVendor (IDFV)** — an Apple-issued identifier specific to this developer's
  apps on the device, reset when they are deleted;
- an **anonymous user id** generated by RevenueCat at first launch — not derived from a name,
  email or Apple Account; the app never calls RevenueCat's login function, so the id is never
  linked to an identity;
- the **purchase and subscription history** for this app: product, start, renewal, trial state;
- basic **device and app information** — device model, OS version, app version, country,
  language.

### 3.2 Advertising measurement
Manifesta asks Apple's **AdServices** framework for an attribution token and passes it to
RevenueCat, to learn whether an Apple Search Ads campaign led to an install and a subscription.
It is aggregated campaign measurement supplied by Apple; it does not identify the user, it is
not the IDFA, and it is not used for profiling or ad targeting.

### 3.3 Purchases
All payments are processed by **Apple** through the App Store. Card details never reach
Manifesta. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that part.

### 3.4 What does not leave the device
Wish texts, WOOP fields, check-in notes, gratitude notes, 369 sessions and voice recordings are
**not sent anywhere**. The app contains no network code of its own: apart from the RevenueCat
calls above, the only outbound links are the ones the user taps — App Store page, this policy,
support page, Apple's standard licence terms — and they open in the browser.

## 4. Services used

| Service | What for | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Apple / App Store | Distribution and payments | Purchase and subscription data, under Apple's own policy |
| RevenueCat, Inc. | Subscription state and attribution | IDFV, anonymous user id, purchase history, device/OS/country, Apple Search Ads attribution token |

That is the complete list. No Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK, no Amplitude or
Mixpanel, no Sentry or Crashlytics, no advertising network.

## 5. Identifiers used
- **IDFV** — sent to RevenueCat; shared only with apps from this same developer, reset when they
  are deleted.
- **RevenueCat anonymous id** — created on the device at first launch, never tied to a name or
  an email.
- **IDFA (advertising identifier)** — **not used.** The app does not link the AdSupport or
  AppTrackingTransparency frameworks.

## 6. Retention
- **On the device:** wishes, recordings, check-ins and settings stay until deleted in the app or
  until the app is deleted. No automatic expiry.
- **At RevenueCat:** the anonymous subscription record is kept as long as the subscription
  relationship needs it, per RevenueCat's own retention practice. Because the record is
  anonymous and no login ever happens, it cannot be traced back by name.
- **At Apple:** purchase records are kept under Apple's terms.

## 7. Tracking, as Apple defines it
Apple defines tracking as linking data with third-party data for advertising or measurement, or
sharing it with a data broker. **Manifesta does neither.** That is why the App Tracking
Transparency prompt is never shown.

## 8. Notifications, widgets and Live Activities
Reminders are scheduled **locally by iOS** at the chosen time. There is no push server, no
device token is registered, and no reminder content is sent anywhere. The Lock Screen candle
(a Live Activity) and the home-screen widgets read the same local database and are equally
offline.

## 9. Microphone
Microphone access is requested only when recording a voice affirmation. The recording is saved
as an audio file in the app's own container and played back from there. **It is never uploaded
and never transcribed by a server.** Denying microphone access leaves the rest of the app
working.

## 10. Deleting your data
Individual wishes, voice notes and check-ins can be deleted inside the app. To remove
everything at once, **delete the app** — that removes the local database, the audio files and
the stored settings.

Manifesta does not yet have a one-tap "delete everything" button or an in-app data export. Make
a copy of anything you want to keep before deleting. We would rather say this plainly than
imply a feature that is not there.

For the anonymous record held by RevenueCat, email us and we will act on it.

## 11. Children
Manifesta is rated 4+ on the App Store, is not directed at children and is not in Apple's Kids
category. No personal detail is asked of anybody.

## 12. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)
Right to know what data is held, to ask for a copy, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict
or object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority — the national data
protection authority in the EU/EEA and the UK, or the KVKK Board in Turkey. In practice most of
this is already in the user's hands, because the data lives on the phone and no account is
held. For anything held by RevenueCat: necatidogrul7@gmail.com, usually answered within 48
hours.

## 13. International transfers
**RevenueCat, Inc.** is based in the United States and operates its infrastructure there. The
subscription data in section 3.1 is therefore transferred outside the EEA, the UK and Turkey,
under RevenueCat's data processing agreement and standard contractual clauses.

expand, it will be said here and in the app.
