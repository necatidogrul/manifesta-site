## The one thing most lists get wrong

Almost every app here sells the pleasant half of a practice that research says only works as a
whole. In Gabriele Oettingen's studies on mental contrasting, people who only imagined the
positive outcome ended up with less energy and effort toward it; the group that imagined the
outcome and then named the obstacle did measurably better, and an if-then plan improved
follow-through further (WOOP: Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan). Affirmation feeds, dream-life
audio and vision boards are the "Wish and Outcome" half. If you want the practice to change what
you do, look for the step where the app makes you write down what will get in the way.

These are also three product types: affirmation feeds (I am, Mantra, Soul), coaching and audio
(Stella, Aya, Vix, Manifest), boards and journals (Vision Board, Myla, Manifesta).

## How to choose

- Biggest and simplest: I am (three quarters of a million ratings, affirmations through the day).
- Hear it, not read it: Stella (generated audio narratives) or Aya (a daily voice persona).
- A picture on your Lock Screen: Vision Board & Goal Tracker, or Myla for the 369 method and meditation in one place.
- The obstacle-and-plan step, in your own voice: Manifesta. It asks what is in the way before you release the wish, and closes the loop when the wish comes true.

## What to check before you subscribe

- Is there an obstacle step? If not, it is a mood app, not a goal app.
- What happens when a wish comes true? Most apps have no "done" state.
- Whose voice? Own-voice recordings versus a synthetic persona.
- Outcome promises: "raise your vibration" or "attract" results cannot be measured. Treat the practice as reflection, not causation.
