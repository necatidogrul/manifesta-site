Manifesta vs Stella — an honest comparison of manifestation apps

By Necati Dogrul, developer of Manifesta · 12 September 2026

Disclosure: written by the developer of one of the two apps. Sections where Stella is stronger are stated explicitly.

Short answer: Stella is the better pick if you want to listen: it generates audio narratives of your goals as if they were already true, and it has by far the larger user base. Manifesta is the better pick if you want a practice with a beginning, an obstacle step and an end: write the wish, name what is in the way, release it in a ritual, hear it in your own voice, and close it when it comes true.

Where Stella is genuinely better

  • Scale. Nearly five thousand ratings in under a year. Manifesta has none in the US yet.
  • Immersion. Personalised, generated audio that describes your specific goals is a strong daily hook, and Stella does nothing else, which is a feature.
  • Zero effort. You do not write anything; you listen. If the obstacle step sounds like homework, Stella is the lighter product.

Where Manifesta is better

  • The obstacle. Before a wish is released, Manifesta asks what is in the way and what you will do when it shows up. This is the part of goal research (mental contrasting, if-then planning) that changes behaviour; positive imagery alone does not.
  • Your voice, not a generated one. Affirmations are recorded by you and replayed to you.
  • A loop that closes. Wishes have a "realised" state. The galaxy changes, and you get a keepsake. Nothing in Stella's listing describes an end state.
  • Presence without opening the app. Widgets, a lock-screen candle and a soft streak keep the intention visible.
  • Turkish. Interface in Turkish as well as English; store listing in 19 languages.

Where they are the same

Both are daily-practice apps with a free tier and a subscription, both are built around your own stated goals, and neither can promise an outcome. Manifesta's listing says so explicitly.

Who should pick which

Pick Stella if you want an immersive audio habit with no writing.

Pick Manifesta if you want a ritual with an obstacle step, your own voice, and a moment of closure when the wish comes true.
