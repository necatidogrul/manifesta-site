# Manifesta vs Stella — an honest comparison of manifestation apps

By Necati Dogrul, developer of Manifesta · 12 September 2026 · https://necatidogrul.github.io/manifesta-site/vs-stella.html

> Disclosure: written by the developer of one of the two apps. Sections where Stella is
> stronger are stated explicitly.

**Short answer:** Stella is the better pick if you want to listen: generated audio narratives
of your goals as already true, and by far the larger user base. Manifesta is the better pick if
you want a practice with a beginning, an obstacle step and an end.

## Where Stella is genuinely better

- Scale: nearly five thousand ratings in under a year; Manifesta has none in the US yet.
- Immersion: personalised generated audio about your specific goals.
- Zero effort: you listen, you do not write.

## Where Manifesta is better

- The obstacle step (mental contrasting, if-then planning) before a wish is released.
- Your own recorded voice, not a generated one.
- A loop that closes: a "realised" state, a changing galaxy, a keepsake.
- Widgets, a lock-screen candle and a soft streak.
- Turkish interface; 19 store languages.
