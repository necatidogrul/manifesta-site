Manifesta — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Türkçe sürüm aşağıda

In short:

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not have them.
  • Your wishes, your obstacle-and-plan notes, your check-ins and your voice recordings stay on your device. There is no iCloud sync and no server of ours that holds them.
  • The only third-party service the app talks to is RevenueCat, and only about subscriptions.
  • No tracking. There is no ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no IDFA. The app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for.
  • Your data is not sold and not passed to data brokers.

1. Who is responsible

Manifesta is published by Necati Doğrul, an independent iOS developer. He is the data controller under the GDPR and the "veri sorumlusu" under Turkey's KVKK. Contact: necatidogrul7@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone. It is stored in a local database inside the app's own App Group container, and it is not mirrored to iCloud or to any server:

  • Wishes and intentions — the wish text and the WOOP fields you fill in: the outcome you want, the obstacle in the way and your small plan; plus the category, the ritual you chose, the state of the wish, the date you created it, any Time Capsule opening date and the gratitude note you add when a wish is realised.
  • Voice affirmations — the title you type and the audio file itself. Recordings are written as ordinary audio files inside the app's container and are played back from there.
  • Check-ins — the daily closeness rating (1–5) and any note you write with it.
  • 369 method sessions — the sessions and the lines you write in them.
  • Settings and counters — your reminder time, the category you picked during onboarding, your streak counters, and the bookkeeping the app uses to decide when not to show you the paywall or the rating prompt again.

There is no account and no profile on any server. If someone else has your unlocked phone, they can read your wishes; that is the trade-off of keeping everything local.

3. What leaves your device

3.1 Subscriptions

Manifesta uses RevenueCat to manage the Premium subscription and to know whether your Premium is active. When the app starts and when you buy, restore or renew, the following is sent to RevenueCat's servers (api.revenuecat.com):

  • the identifierForVendor (IDFV) — an Apple-issued identifier that is specific to this developer's apps on your device and resets when you delete them;
  • an anonymous user id generated by RevenueCat on first launch — it is not derived from your name, email or Apple Account, and the app never calls RevenueCat's login function, so this id is never linked to an identity;
  • your purchase and subscription history for this app: which product, when it started, whether it renewed, whether it is in a trial;
  • basic device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, country and language.

3.2 Advertising measurement

Manifesta asks Apple's AdServices framework for an attribution token and passes it to RevenueCat. This is how we learn whether an Apple Search Ads campaign led to an install and to a subscription. It is aggregated campaign measurement provided by Apple; it does not identify you, it is not the IDFA, and it is not used to build a profile or to target ads.

3.3 Purchases

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Card details never reach Manifesta; we never see them. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that part.

3.4 What does not leave your device

Your wish texts, WOOP fields, check-in notes, gratitude notes, 369 sessions and voice recordings are not sent anywhere. The app contains no network code of its own: apart from the RevenueCat calls described above, the only outbound links are the ones you tap — the App Store page, this policy, the support page and Apple's standard licence terms, all of which open in your browser.

5. Identifiers we use

  • IDFV — sent to RevenueCat as described above. It is shared only with apps from this same developer and is reset when you delete them.
  • RevenueCat anonymous id — created on your device at first launch, never tied to a name or an email.
  • IDFA (advertising identifier) — not used. The app does not link the AdSupport or AppTrackingTransparency frameworks, so there is nothing to ask permission for.

6. Retention

  • On your device: your wishes, recordings, check-ins and settings stay until you delete them in the app or delete the app itself. There is no automatic expiry.
  • At RevenueCat: the anonymous subscription record is kept for as long as the subscription relationship needs it, in line with RevenueCat's own retention practice. Because the record is anonymous and we never log you in, it cannot be traced back to you by name.
  • At Apple: purchase records are kept under Apple's terms, not ours.

7. Tracking, as Apple defines it

Apple defines tracking as linking your data with third-party data for advertising or measurement, or sharing it with a data broker. Manifesta does neither. That is why the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt: there is no tracking to ask you about.

8. Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS at the time you choose. There is no push server, no device token is registered, and no reminder content is sent anywhere. The Lock Screen candle (a Live Activity) and the home-screen widgets read the same local database and are equally offline.

9. Microphone

Manifesta asks for microphone access only when you record a voice affirmation. The recording is saved as an audio file in the app's own container on your device and is played back from there. It is never uploaded and never transcribed by a server. If you deny microphone access, every other part of the app keeps working.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete an individual wish, voice note or check-in inside the app. To remove everything at once, delete the app — that removes the local database, the audio files and the stored settings from your phone.

Manifesta does not yet have a one-tap "delete everything" button or a data export inside the app. If you want a copy of what you have written before deleting, write it down or take screenshots first. We would rather say this plainly than imply a feature that is not there.

To exercise a right over the anonymous record held by RevenueCat, email us and we will act on it.

11. Children

Manifesta is rated 4+ on the App Store, is not directed at children and is not part of Apple's Kids category. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the app asks for no personal detail from anybody.

12. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

You have the right to know what data is held about you, to ask for a copy, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — your national data protection authority in the EU/EEA and the UK, or the KVKK Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu) in Turkey.

In practice most of this is in your hands already, because the data lives on your phone and we hold no account for you. For anything held by RevenueCat, email necatidogrul7@gmail.com and we will handle the request. We normally reply within 48 hours.

13. International transfers

RevenueCat, Inc. is based in the United States and operates its infrastructure there. The subscription data described in section 3.1 is therefore transferred outside the EEA, the UK and Turkey. The transfer takes place under RevenueCat's data processing agreement and standard contractual clauses.

14. Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the kinds of data the app collects ever expand, we will say so here and in the app.
